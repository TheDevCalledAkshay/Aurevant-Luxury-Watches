The Collection

Three hours.
Twenty-four each.

Each reference is named for a moment of the day and limited to twenty-four pieces. When an edition is complete, the dies are retired.

Ref. I

Nox

The night watch. A midnight enamel dial that swallows light, offset by the warm blush of 18k rose gold. The moonphase complication tracks the sky with a margin of one day in 122 years.

Diameter40 mm
DialMidnight Grand Feu Enamel
MovementCal. AV.01 · Manual
Power Reserve72 Hours
